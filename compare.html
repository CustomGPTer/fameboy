What Fame Boy does not do

Fame Boy has no link-cable multiplayer, no picture shaders and no tilt, solar or rumble support, and it does not play DS games. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where they do more

My Boy! Lite has link-cable play over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, tilt, solar and rumble support, and picture shaders. Pizza Boy A has shaders and RetroAchievements, and its Pro version adds multiplayer and Google Drive sync. Both install and update from Google Play.
