Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the Fame Boy app for Android (package uk.jj.fameboy) and this website. Fame Boy is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Your games, saves, play time and settings stay on your phone. They are never uploaded to the developer.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • Fame Boy goes online only for a cover you ask it to download, after you switch that on, and when you tap Check now under Check for updates.

What stays on your phone

Fame Boy keeps copies of the games you add, their saves and save states, covers, cheats, your library details, controller layouts and settings in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's cloud backup is switched off for Fame Boy, so none of this is copied to Google. If you choose to link a game in your own folder instead of copying it, the file stays where it is and Fame Boy reads it from there.

When Fame Boy goes online

WhatWhat it sendsTo
Cover downloadThe game's title, when you ask for that game's cover. Off until you switch it on in Settings.The libretro thumbnails collection on GitHub
Check for updatesA request for the newest version of Fame Boy, only when you tap Check now. Nothing about you or your games.GitHub

Like any website, these services see your phone's internet address when it connects, under GitHub's privacy statement. Download and website links open in your own browser.

What the app can reach

AccessWhy

BiometricsOptional fingerprint unlock for the app lock

Fame Boy does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone or phone.

Backups

A backup leaves the phone only when you save it, to the place you choose. If you set a password, the file is sealed with AES-256-GCM using a key made from your password with PBKDF2 (210,000 rounds); without the password nobody, including the developer, can open it. Your app lock PIN is never put in a backup.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can remove any game, with or without its saves, and uninstalling Fame Boy deletes everything it holds. Export a backup first if you want to keep your progress.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands on your phone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. The only script on it unlocks the download once the lawful-use box is ticked. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Fame Boy collects nothing from anyone. Younger users should ask a parent before adding games or switching on cover downloads.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 26 November 2026.
